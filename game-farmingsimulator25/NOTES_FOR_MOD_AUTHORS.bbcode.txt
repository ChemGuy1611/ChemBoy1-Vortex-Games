[b][color=#e69138][size=4]Notes for Mod Authors - Farming Simulator 25[/size]
[/color][/b]Packaging rules for Farming Simulator 25 mods, so Vortex installs them to the right place.

Vortex decides what a mod is by looking at the files and folders inside the archive. It tries each installer in order and the first one that matches wins, so archive layout is what determines where your mod ends up.

========================================================================

Zip:

Handled by the "testZip" installer. Inspect the extension source for the exact archive layout it expects.

========================================================================

Rules That Apply To Every Mod Type:

    - Archives containing a FOMOD installer (a "fomod" folder with ModuleConfig.xml) are handed to the built-in FOMOD installer instead.
    - Folder and file name matching is case-insensitive.
    - Extra wrapper folders around a recognised folder are generally fine.
